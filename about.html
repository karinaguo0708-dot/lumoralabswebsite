About Lumora

Create. Inspire. Impact.

LUMORA LABS is an indie studio exploring games, education, psychology, and meaningful digital experiences.

Studio belief

Games can make complicated systems feel human.

We are interested in the gap between what people say and what situations ask of them. Our projects use playable decisions to make that gap visible.

That means smaller experiences, sharper scenarios, and systems that invite players to reflect after the screen fades.

The principles that guide everything we build.
